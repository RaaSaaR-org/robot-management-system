| Item | Definition |
|---|---|

| Primary tagline | **Intelligence. Made physical.** |
| Built by | EmAI Robotics GmbH, Saarbrücken, Germany |
| Personality | Intelligent, trustworthy, calm, professional, minimal |

Write **NeoDEM** as one word, with capital N, D, E, and M. Use the product name for the platform and EmAI Robotics GmbH when identifying the company behind it.

The name's creative origin combines “Neo” with “Deus Ex Machina.” Use **Neo Data & Execution Management** as the expansion in product communication. The origin story is optional background.

## Product definition

NeoDEM connects robot data, models, and machines in one workspace. It brings together the Physical AI lifecycle: collecting demonstrations, training models, managing deployment, evaluating behavior, operating fleets, and keeping operational records.

The platform coordinates modular services. Training, inference, and simulation may require separate services and hardware; available capabilities depend on the deployment and robot integration.

## Brand promise

Give robotics teams a connected workflow and control over their data, models, and machines, with clear evidence of what the system can do.

## The Embodied Loop

**Collect → Train → Deploy → Evaluate → Operate → Comply → Collect.**

The loop is the central product story: operational experience informs the next dataset, model, and evaluation. Its stages have different readiness levels, which must remain visible in demonstrations and product claims.

## Product scope

The architecture is designed for different robot embodiments. Development focuses on cognitive humanoids, particularly the Unitree G1; the SO-101 arm is an existing integration reference. A robot pictured in brand material does not establish hardware support. Describe support by model, configuration, capability, and validation status.
