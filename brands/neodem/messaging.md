## One-sentence description

NeoDEM is an open, modular platform for Physical AI that connects robot data, model workflows, and fleet operations in one workspace.

## Short description

NeoDEM helps robotics teams connect demonstrations, datasets, models, and machines. It brings collection, training, deployment, evaluation, operations, and record-keeping into a shared workflow. Teams retain control over their infrastructure and choose compatible robot and model integrations, with readiness made explicit for each configuration.

## Extended description

NeoDEM is an open, modular platform for Physical AI, built by EmAI Robotics GmbH. It connects the work of robotics engineers, AI teams, and fleet operators through the Embodied Loop: Collect, Train, Deploy, Evaluate, Operate, and Comply.

Teams can organize demonstrations and datasets, coordinate model workflows, inspect evaluations, and manage integrated robots from a shared workspace. Open formats and modular services keep data and infrastructure choices in the team's hands. Hardware support and workflow readiness are stated explicitly, so a simulation result remains distinguishable from a validated physical deployment.

## Message for robot manufacturers

Bring your robot into a connected Physical AI workflow. We integrate your robot's supported controls, telemetry, sensors, and model description into NeoDEM, then validate the agreed capabilities on your hardware. Advanced teleoperation, data collection, and learned skills can extend the integration around your use cases.

## Messaging consistency

Lead with the primary tagline and the modular Physical AI category. The runtime [brand defaults](../../app/src/brand/defaults.ts) still contain “Fleet Management System” and “Bringing Intelligence to Motion.” Those existing UI strings differ from the current [brand contract](../../docs/brand.md) and [landing hero](../../app/src/components/landing/HeroSection.tsx); use the current brand contract for new communication.

When using phrases such as “any model” or “every embodiment,” include the applicable compatibility and integration scope. Reuse measured performance claims only with their evidence and test conditions.
